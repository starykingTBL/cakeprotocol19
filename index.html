hey

Once upon a time, a young lad was minding his business on Snapchat.

Not looking for anyone.
Not expecting anything.
Just… there.

Then the algorithm — being the sneaky little matchmaker it is — decided to slide a profile his way. No drumroll. No big intro. Just a name and a vibe that said “Me😌❤️” — like that was reason enough.

He looked at it. He thought about it. He added her.

She was somewhere in the UK. He was wherever he was. Two different worlds. Same timeline. Same app. Same moment.

She accepted.
He played it cool and didn’t text that day.
Very classic of him.

✦

But then.

She posted a horror video on her story.

He had literally just finished watching one.

Something about that felt less like coincidence and more like a setup. So he slid into her DMs. He doesn’t remember exactly what he said — but the energy? That, he remembers. Like two people who’d somehow known each other longer than they actually had.

They talked about horror. Then books. Then life — the real kind. The kind of conversation that sneaks up on you, and suddenly it’s 3am and neither of you wants to stop.

✦

Then came the call.

She says it was a mistake — she called by accident.
He picked up anyway.
And what was supposed to be a two-second “oops” turned into something that changed everything.

He saved her as Okomi 🤍
She saved him as Iyawomi 🤍

✦

And somewhere between the inside jokes, the late-night calls, the “you be fish” moments (he started it, by the way), and the conversations that hit different when they come from the right person —

Something real happened.

He stopped just passing time.
She stopped just talking to someone.

And without either of them planning it, without a script or a perfect moment —

They became each other’s favourite part of the day.

…and that’s our love story. That’s how he found the most beautiful girl in his world.

Happy 19th Birthday

Omosile 🎂

June 10, 2007.

Somewhere in a hospital, your mum was calling your dad — “Otinbo!! Yee!!” 🤣 And I can only imagine the chaos. Emmy and Aunty Mary somewhere in that building, fully losing their minds. Your dad pacing those corridors like a man on a mission — quietly making Jesus his personal best friend that day.

Then it happened.

Kewn. Kewn. Kewn. 🤣

The sound nobody in that room was ready for, but everybody needed.

They cleaned you up. You rested. You slept — honestly, very on-brand for you. And then a week later, they looked at this tiny little person and said: “Mother of all Nations.” I’m almost certain that was your mum’s idea.

And then came Omosile.

The name I love most. Because you really did open something in me — but that’s a conversation for another day.

Happy Birthday, baby girl.

Enjoy every single second of today.
You deserve it more than you know.
You deserve all of it — the love, the joy,
the big moments and the quiet ones.

I love you so much, Omosile.

And I hope that one day — when the time is right and the stars align — it’s you I get to call mine. Forever.
