Chapter V

As he sees her.

I wasn't even supposed to be on Snapchat that day.

I don't know what made me open the app. I don't know what made me stop at your profile. I don't know what made me add someone I didn't know, in a country I've never been to, with a name that just said "Me😌❤️" like that was enough of a reason.

But it was.

You posted a horror video on your story.
I had just finished watching one.
I said something — I don't even remember what.
But I remember the energy.
I remember thinking "this person is different."

Then you called.

You said it was a mistake.

Okomi — that was the best mistake you've ever made.

We talked about horror.
Then books.
Then life.
Then everything.
Then nothing — just talking because stopping felt wrong.

I've called you okomi.
You've called me Iyawomi.
I don't know when this became the realest thing in my life but it did.

Your father named you Omosile because you completed the house when you arrived.

I believe that.

Because ever since you arrived in my world —
something in me that I didn't know was unfinished
finally felt complete.

I'm not good at grand gestures.
I'm not good at perfect timing.
But I built this.
Every scene. Every word. Every star you collected.
All of it was just me trying to say one thing
that I keep almost saying and never do.

OMOSILE —

I love you.

Not the casual kind.
The kind that made me build a whole website
on a phone
just so the moment I said it
would feel as big as it actually is.

So.

Will you be my person?
Officially. For real.
